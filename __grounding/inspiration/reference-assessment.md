## Primary structural anchor: reference 09

- **09 — [`09-tactical-gear-landing-page-anorent.png`](references/dribbble/09-tactical-gear-landing-page-anorent.png)**
  - **Source:** *tactical gear landing page \ anorent®* by Fantik Studio® ([dribbble.com/shots/26831532](https://dribbble.com/shots/26831532-tactical-gear-landing-page-anorent), query `"topographic interface"`, 146k views, 541 likes, fetched `2026-10-25`).
  - **Observed structure:** Full-bleed warm ash surface (`#DFDFE0`) etched with low-contrast organic topographic contour lines (`#D1D1D5`). Perimeter navigation is pushed to the outer edges: a compact top bar, a floating mid-left vertical navigation column where the selected row (`| CAMO`) uses a sharp left vertical bar and high-contrast near-black type while sibling items sit in muted grey above a 2×10 dot-matrix block, a commanding central 3D specimen with large editorial stage titles above and ghosted neighboring stages vertically aligned, and a restrained mid-right customization column using circular swatches, pill selectors, and a dotted divider.
  - **What we adopt and reject:** We adopt the spatial architecture—clicky left vertical navigation rail with a left bar indicator, quiet topographic ash background, generous negative space, commanding central stage (the smoky build chamber in Heavy Rain System or the exposed 3D AUGI Sable chassis in AUGI Engineering), and a restrained right parameter/worker rail. We reject all military equipment subject matter.
